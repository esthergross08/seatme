# SeatMe Instagram — Week 1 Shot List & Captions

Drafted 2026-08-18, companion to `INSTAGRAM_LAUNCH_CAMPAIGN.md`. Everything below is designed for zero budget: one phone, natural light, and things you already own (paper, a pen, a laptop). Nothing requires a second person, a location, or anything purchased — though a couple of posts are more fun with a friend roped in for 30 seconds if one's around.

## Before you shoot: the free setup

Shoot vertical (9:16), in portrait lock. Face a window for light instead of using an overhead light — free and better than any ring light. Prop the phone against a stack of books or a mug instead of a tripod. Edit in Instagram's own Reels editor or CapCut (both free) — text overlays, cuts, and captions can all be done there, no separate software needed. For trending audio: open Reels → the audio/create tab → sort by trending, and pick something in the "chaotic/frantic" or "deadpan relatable" vibe close to when you actually post, since trending sounds rotate fast and a specific one named today may be stale by the time you film — the vibe matters more than the exact track.

## Post 1 — Intro Reel: "things only people who've made a wedding seating chart understand"

**Format:** Text-over-b-roll Reel, faceless, ~10-15 seconds. No acting needed — this is just quick shots of the *evidence* of seating chart chaos.

**Shot list (all phone, all free):**
1. A blank piece of paper or a fresh whiteboard/notes app — 1 second, establishing "before."
2. Overhead shot of you actually scribbling names, crossing them out, drawing arrows between "these two CANNOT be near each other." Real footage of real messy handwriting is the whole joke — don't over-produce this.
3. A shot of a laptop screen with 30 browser tabs open (wedding forums, spreadsheets, Pinterest) — even better if this is genuinely your screen right now.
4. Crumpled paper being tossed, or a screen being closed in defeat.
5. Final shot: you (or just the pen) dropping onto the desk, done.

**On-screen text (one line appears per shot, synced to cuts):**
- "no one warns you about this part"
- "it's not even that many people"
- "why does everyone hate each other suddenly"
- "who put the exes at the same table"
- "we've been doing this for THREE HOURS"

**Caption:**
"things they don't tell you about wedding planning, part 1: the seating chart will humble you. 🪑💀 tag someone who's currently in the trenches"

**Hashtags:** #weddingplanning #seatingchart #engaged #bridetobe #weddingtok #familydrama

## Post 2 — Carousel: "Seating Chart Difficulty Tiers"

**Format:** Static image carousel, 5-6 slides, built entirely in Canva (free tier is enough — no camera involved at all).

**Slide list:**
1. Title slide: "Rate your seating chart difficulty" with the SeatMe gold/cream palette.
2. "Tier 1: 15 people, backyard BBQ" — subtext: "you could do this blindfolded"
3. "Tier 2: 80 guests, one divorced couple, everyone still gets along" — subtext: "mildly spicy"
4. "Tier 3: 150 guests, two feuding sides of the family, a friend-group breakup, and a toddler who bites" — subtext: "this is a war crime"
5. "Tier 4: all of the above, plus your future mother-in-law 'just has a few thoughts'" — subtext: "send help"
6. Closer slide: "which tier are you on? 👀 comment below"

**Caption:**
"be honest, which tier is your seating chart 😅 comment your number, we need to know how bad it is out there"

**Hashtags:** #weddingplanning #seatingchart #bridetobe #weddinghumor #engaged

**Design tip:** keep every slide the same template (same fonts/colors, just swap text) — it reads as a series and takes 15 minutes total once the first slide is built.

## Post 3 — POV Reel: the maid of honor's face when the chart changes again

**Format:** Selfie-mode reaction Reel, ~6-8 seconds, one take. This one does need a face on camera — yours, or rope in a friend/partner for literally 30 seconds. If nobody's available to be on camera, skip this one this week and swap in another faceless post from the calendar; don't force it.

**Shot list:**
1. Hold the phone selfie-style. Start with a neutral/bored expression.
2. Text on screen appears: "the bride: ok wait what if we move EVERYONE again"
3. Cut to your reaction — the "I have died inside but am smiling" face. Big exaggerated blink, forced smile, maybe a slow nod. Overacting is the joke here, not subtlety.
4. Hold for a beat, then cut.

**Caption:**
"the maid of honor after the seating chart gets rearranged for the fifth time 🙃 no one talks about this labor"

**Hashtags:** #maidofhonor #weddingplanning #bridalparty #weddingtok #relatable

## Post 4 — UGC bait: "tag the friend currently losing it over their seating chart"

**Format:** Single static image post, Canva, no camera needed.

**Design:** Simple, bold centered text on the SeatMe cream/gold background — no photo needed at all. Something like: "tag the friend who is currently three hours deep into a seating chart spreadsheet and has not eaten today"

**Caption:**
"you know exactly who this is. tag them. let's see how many people we can call out 👇"

**Hashtags:** #weddingplanning #seatingchart #tagafriend #bridetobe

**Why this works:** tags and shares are the strongest signal for reaching new people right now — this is designed purely to generate that, not to look impressive on its own.

## Post 5 — "Non-planners think it's cute. Planners know it's advanced geometry."

**Format:** Can go either way — simplest version is a static quote-card image (Canva, zero camera), or a slightly punchier version as a 5-second Reel if you want the variety.

**Static version:** Large centered text, two lines, big contrast between them:
Line 1 (smaller, muted): "people who haven't planned a wedding: 'aw, a seating chart, so cute'"
Line 2 (larger, bold, gold): "people who have: this is advanced geometry"

**Reel version (if you want video instead):**
1. Shot of a calm, smiling person saying (as text, or lip-synced to trending audio): "aw a seating chart, that's so cute"
2. Hard cut to you surrounded by scribbled paper, looking unhinged, text: "IT'S ADVANCED GEOMETRY, KAREN"

**Caption:**
"there is no in-between. you either haven't done it yet, or you have the thousand-yard stare 😵‍💫"

**Hashtags:** #weddingplanning #seatingchart #engaged #weddinghumor #bridetobe

Nothing, on purpose — none of the five posts above mention the product. Week 1 is entirely about establishing the voice and getting the first shares.
